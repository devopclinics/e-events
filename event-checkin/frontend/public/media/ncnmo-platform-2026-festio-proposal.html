The opportunity

A gathering this rich deserves one operating picture.

Platform 2026 brings families, youth, scholars, professionals, and community leaders together in Indianapolis for five days of learning, worship, service, recreation, and connection. With parallel programs and ten age groups, the experience must feel simple to every guest while giving the planning team reliable information in real time.

For the NCNMO workflow, a guest must be checked into the convention before a room or activity movement can be recorded. This keeps the arrival count distinct from session and zone attendance.

Junior Platform

Safeguarding designed into the handoff

Each child has an individual credential. Each protected handoff combines the child’s badge, the selected Junior zone, the movement direction, and an authorized guardian credential.

Explicit authorization. Household membership alone does not grant pickup rights.
Two-credential verification. Staff scan the guardian and child for configured junior movements.
Repeat visits. IN → OUT → IN → OUT can be retained throughout the day.
Safe state transitions. Duplicate entry, duplicate exit, and exit without entry are denied and audited.
Accountability. Reports include child, guardian, relationship, zone, direction, time, decision, and operator.

Attendance operating plan

Track concurrent sessions with phones already on hand

Festio runs in a browser and can use the camera on a suitable smartphone or tablet. NCNMO does not need to purchase dedicated barcode scanners for the proposed workflow.

A

Priority tracking

Track arrival, Junior Platform, meals, souvenirs, competitions, and selected high-value sessions. This provides strong insight with the smallest volunteer footprint.

B

Full room tracking

Assign one trained room ambassador with a phone at each tracked entrance. Parallel sessions require parallel operators during the arrival window.

C

Managed support

Festio can supply an agreed on-site lead or scanning team as an optional service when NCNMO does not want to staff every checkpoint.

The practical recommendation

Use NCNMO volunteers with their existing phones for most checkpoints, supported by one Festio operations lead during rehearsal and peak periods. Track every critical entitlement and safeguarding movement, then select the sessions where person-level attendance will materially help planning.

Delivery approach
