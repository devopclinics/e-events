Staff-only guidance for safe, calm child movements. Volunteers keep every handoff safe and smooth to prevent overcrowding. Use the same Festio account and the event assigned by your Admin. Parents do not operate Festio Juniors.

  3. Search or scan the child in front of you, verify the details, then confirm the next action.

ACCESS & NON-NEGOTIABLE CHECKS

Two roles; clear responsibilities

AdminFull Festio Juniors access within the account's authorized scope. Can add staff, assign permissions and manage the event's Juniors settings.
VolunteerOnly selected operational permissions. Own shifts and the guide remain available. Existing view-only limits still apply.

Permissions an Admin can assign

  • Check-in, pickup & movements
  • Add/correct children & guardians
  • Support desks & restricted notes
  • Create & manage outings
  • Manage inventory
  • Guardian contacts & notification history
  • Coordinate volunteers & shifts
  • Resolve previous-day movement records
More → Volunteers → Volunteers tab

Select one or several people → Set role & permissions → choose the permissions → Save.

Remember every day

  • Platform/event admission is not JP custody.
  • Every JP day starts with drop-off and today's screening/band confirmation.
  • Allergy and accommodation facts can prefill; daily clearance does not.
  • Every temporary departure needs its own return.
  • Collector authorization and physical identity verification are separate.

01 · Orientation

Start on your phone

Home, Children, Scan, My shift and More.

  1. Open festio.events/juniors/ and sign in with your existing account. New staff create and verify an account using the exact email assigned by the Admin. Choose the correct event.
  2. Home shows your shift, scanning, search and attention counts. Children opens search; Scan opens the camera; My shift opens your personal assignments. Find Outings, Support desks, Notifications, Inventory and permitted Admin tools under More.
  3. Use the theme button for light or dark mode. Open Account & app to install or sign out. On iPhone/iPad use Safari Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android use the browser's Install app option.
STOP & CHECK

Admin is full Juniors access within its assigned scope; Volunteer receives only checked permissions. Existing event/organization Admins inherit the appropriate access. A missing tool may be a permission restriction. Stay online: handoffs are not queued offline.

02 · Check-in

Find the child once

Guest List owns the shared child and guardian identities.

  1. Search by child name, classroom or badge reference, or scan their existing badge. The general child roster stays hidden until a search; an explicitly opened support or movement queue shows its relevant children.
  2. Scanning identifies and opens a record. It does not automatically record attendance, a movement or pickup. Confirm the child and the next action on screen.
  3. Missing or incorrect record? An Admin or volunteer with Add/correct children & guardians permission adds or corrects the shared record. Search first and avoid duplicates.
STOP & CHECK

A badge identifies a child. It never proves that the adult present is authorized to collect them.

03 · Check-in

Separate Platform check-in from daily drop-off

Follow this event's configured JP start date.

  1. For NCNMO Junior Platform 2026, Day 0 is Platform check-in; from Day 1, record daily JP drop-off. Follow the configured start date for other events. Platform check-in alone does not begin JP custody or complete screening.
  2. Every JP day begins with a fresh drop-off after event admission. The linked guardian is prefilled; choose the adult actually present. For Another person, record their name, relationship and optional international phone number.
  3. Verify the drop-off in person. Verify a sibling is 18+. Complete today's screening confirmation and physically issue/check the standard band before confirming drop-off.
STOP & CHECK

Yesterday's unresolved custody must be reconciled by an authorized lead before a new daily drop-off. A drop-off adult is not automatically a pickup-authorized collector; review that authorization separately.

04 · Check-in

Confirm saved screening answers

Reuse information; confirm today's facts.

  1. At drop-off or screening, supported child-specific registration answers or previous screening answers are prefilled. The source is shown. A parent's own answers are not copied to a child, and another event's answers are not used.
  2. Ask and complete any missing or unknown allergy/accommodation answer. Confirm saved answers with the adult today and change anything that is different. Tick I confirmed both screening answers with the adult today.
  3. Every screened child needs the standard screening wristband before classroom arrival, including children with No for both needs. A Yes answer also sends the child to the corresponding support desk; both Yes answers require both reviews.
STOP & CHECK

Saved answers do not mean today's desk reviews or wristbands are complete. Daily clearances and band confirmations do not automatically carry forward. Do not assume a missing answer means No.

05 · Support

Clear every required desk

One standard band; additional bands for additional needs.

  1. A volunteer with Support desks & restricted notes permission reviews each referral. Relevant saved details can prefill the restricted notes; review and update them before confirming.
  2. Confirm the physical band for each applicable need. Standard only = one band; standard plus allergy OR accommodation = two; standard plus both = three.
  3. Clear both referrals when both apply. Classroom arrival remains blocked until required reviews, bands and classroom assignment are complete.
STOP & CHECK

Medical details stay restricted to authorized staff. Do not copy them into guardian messages or general movement notes. A saved form never grants automatic desk clearance.

06 · Check-in

Record actual classroom arrival

Headcounts follow the recorded location.

  1. Confirm the assigned classroom. If missing, ask an Admin or a volunteer with child-record permission to set it in the child's shared record.
  2. Check the standard band and all applicable extra bands. Finish each support-desk review first.
  3. Confirm classroom arrival when the child physically reaches the classroom. Reception, support desk and classroom are separate locations.
STOP & CHECK

Do not record a child in a classroom while they are still waiting at reception or a support desk.

07 · Check-in

Complete Clinic and Other return cycles

Departure → return to JP → classroom or verified pickup.

  1. Choose Temporary departure: clinic or Temporary departure: other. Record the accompanying adult; Other also requires an internal reason. The account and time are recorded with the movement.
  2. When the child is physically back, choose Return from clinic / other. With no recorded return, the child remains away in the headcount.
  3. If resuming class, record classroom arrival. If leaving for the day, record the return first, then complete verified final pickup.
STOP & CHECK

Never use final release for a clinic visit or assume a child returned. Clinic, Other and Outing each need their own recorded return.

08 · Coordination

Account for each outing attendee

Expected, departed, returned and awaiting-return counts.

  1. Under More → Outings, an Admin or permitted outing coordinator creates the named, dated roster. Search/select children, verify outing permission and create the outing. Changing the search keeps selected children.
  2. A volunteer with movement permission opens the outing and uses Not departed, Awaiting return, Returned or All attendees, with attendee search. Scan outing badge or open the child. Confirm departure for that exact outing only on its scheduled date, after the child is in class.
  3. Record each return when the child is physically back; the child returns to JP reception. Confirm classroom arrival if resuming the day. Review every outstanding child before closing the outing.
STOP & CHECK

Scanning opens a confirmation; it never moves a child automatically. A child not yet dropped off must complete the arrival steps first. Close reconciled outing is blocked while any departed child lacks a return.

09 · Check-in

Authorize, verify and release

Collector permission and the physical identity check are separate.

  1. If No authorized adult collector appears, drop-off, screening and classroom steps can continue; final pickup remains blocked. An Admin or guardian-editor uses Review collector authorization, or Add authorized collector when no contact exists.
  2. Review the linked adult or enter the correct guardian, verify adulthood and mark Authorized to collect this child. Admin access already includes guardian editing. To delegate it: More → Volunteers → Volunteers tab → select people → Set role & permissions → Add/correct children & guardians → Save.
  3. At final pickup, choose an already-authorized, adult-verified collector. Verify the person present against that authorization and confirm the physical handoff before Confirm verified pickup. A different, unapproved collector needs authorized staff review first. Call/Email shortcuts, when permitted, open the device’s phone or email app.
STOP & CHECK

A drop-off record, typed name or badge alone cannot authorize pickup. Never bypass missing authorization. Return children from Clinic, Other or Outing before final release.

10 · Coordination

Read communication statuses correctly

Recording a movement does not guarantee a delivered message.

  1. Guardian email/SMS notices are recorded for movements. Actual sending depends on the event sending switch, designated guardian contacts, SMS permission, provider setup and available credits.
  2. Queued means waiting; Accepted means the provider accepted the message; Delivered requires a receipt. Missing setup, failures and uncertain outcomes need review before retrying.
  3. Use Notifications when permitted. Practice events never send guardian or volunteer messages. Admins can review volunteer access emails, shift reminders and handover reporting in Volunteers → Coverage & messages.
STOP & CHECK

Do not promise a message was delivered because a movement saved or the provider accepted it. Restricted medical details are excluded from guardian transition messages.

11 · Inventory

Record supplies issued and returned

Use the current inventory form accurately.

  1. Open Inventory and the correct item. Check available quantity before issuing supplies.
  2. Record the action, quantity and responsible volunteer. Put the destination, such as Room A, and useful handover details in Note; the current form has no separate destination selector.
  3. Record good-condition returns, damaged returns and lost issued items separately. Check the issue/return history. Only mark items returned when physically received.
STOP & CHECK

Inventory totals are not proof that a particular classroom returned its allocation. Keep clear responsibility and destination notes; reconcile against the physical stock.

12 · Coordination

Arrive, count and hand over together

Volunteers keep the whole programme moving safely.

  1. An Admin opens More → Volunteers → Volunteers tab to add names, emails and optional phones in table rows. Choose Admin or Volunteer and permissions, save, then select people for classroom and shift assignments. Email updates can be requested; delivery is reported separately.
  2. Open My shift and check in only when physically at the station. Before leaving, count the children together and review Clinic, Other, Outing and unresolved records. Offer handover to the assigned incoming volunteer.
  3. The incoming volunteer checks in and accepts the current physical count. The outgoing volunteer then finishes the shift. Use Shift planner for reminder timing, repeat intervals, exact times and supported email/SMS/WhatsApp/MMS channels; non-email channels need appropriate contact/consent/provider readiness.
STOP & CHECK

Do not leave children without relief. An overdue shift remains on duty until checkout. Reminders are not attendance, and accepted email is not a delivery receipt. Finish active handovers before changing access or removing classroom assignments.

ORIENTATION PRACTICE

Run the exceptions before event day

Use the separate practice event with fictional contacts. Stay online. Practice never sends guardian or volunteer messages.

  • Find a child without showing the whole roster
  • NCNMO Day 0 Platform check-in, followed by daily JP drop-off from Day 1
  • Prefilled Yes/No and missing screening answers
  • A child needing all three bands
  • Admin collector authorization and verified pickup
  • Clinic departure, return and classroom arrival
  • Other departure, return and classroom arrival
  • Outing departure and a missing return
  • Incoming/outgoing physical shift handover
  • Paused practice messages and missing notification setup
Before the first real shift

Know your station, assigned classroom, lead contact, incoming relief, event timezone and where to refer a child for support or identity corrections.
