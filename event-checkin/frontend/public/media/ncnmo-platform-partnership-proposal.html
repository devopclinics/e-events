What we're asking for

A real partnership runs both ways

We're not asking NCNMO to pay for what Platform deserves — we're asking for the kind of relationship that makes this worth doing for us too.

  • 1

    Be our reference partner.

    Permission to name NCNMO and Platform in our own materials — with your approval on anything before it's published.

  • 2

    Tell us honestly how it went.

    A short conversation after Platform 2026, in your words, that we can turn into a case study.

  • 3

    An introduction, if it feels right.

    NCNMO also runs ICARE, YASR, ASPIRE, and its Medical Mission — an introduction to whoever organizes those is welcome, never required.

  • 4

    Your committee's feedback, direct to our team.

    What a five-day faith-community gathering actually needs — nobody teaches us that better than the people running one.
