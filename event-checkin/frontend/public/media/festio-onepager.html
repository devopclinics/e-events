The complete event workflow from invite to door to table.

Festio helps organizers create branded invite pages, collect RSVPs, send WhatsApp/SMS/email invites, check guests in by QR, manage venue access, seating, orders, deliveries, and gift lists, and watch live Results — one pay-per-event platform.

What it replaces

Invites & RSVP

Open links or personal invite links, approvals, deadlines, custom questions, and broadcast updates.

Check-in

Browser-based QR check-in for staff — no app install — with offline caching and a live audit trail.

Venue access

Zones, gates, ticket types and tags, occupancy limits, and guest-journey analytics.

Seating & orders

Tables, partner pairing, meal/drink/gift choices, and a live kitchen order queue.

Deliveries

Aso-ebi, merch, welcome bags — vendor packing lists, addresses, and fulfilment status.

Gift list

Wishlist items and cash funds, claims, and direct giving — no money handled by Festio.
