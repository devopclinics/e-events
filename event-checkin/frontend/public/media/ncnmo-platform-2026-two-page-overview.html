DELIVERY & OPERATING MODEL
PLATFORM 2026
Practical implementation

A lean rollout with proof before scale

The recommended model limits equipment costs, uses NCNMO’s existing team where practical, and gives the committee evidence before committing to the full convention.

1Demonstrate

Review the configured staging journey, dashboards, prior-event screenshots, badges, reports, and approved customer references.

2Pilot at the AGM

Run a limited real-event experience with agreed success criteria, training, communications, engagement, and reporting.

3Scale by approval

Use the pilot findings to finalize Platform 2026. Any future ICARE deployment remains optional and separately scoped.

Recommended balance

Track every critical checkpoint—arrival, Junior Platform, meals, souvenirs, competitions, and Gala Night—then select the sessions where person-level attendance will materially improve planning. Tracking every simultaneous room is possible, but it requires an operator at every tracked entrance. Festio can quote an optional managed scanning team.

Evidence, not experimentation

Use approved prior-event dashboards, measurable outcomes, and customer testimonials. Remove personal information and obtain permission for every named quote or reference.

Commercial clarity

Separate software and configuration, printed badges, messaging usage, optional equipment, and optional Festio staffing. Final amounts follow the scope workshop.

Recommended next decision

Approve a working session to confirm the AGM pilot, priority attendance checkpoints, Junior policy, communications, badge format, NCNMO volunteer roles, optional Festio support, and the success measures used to decide on Platform 2026.
